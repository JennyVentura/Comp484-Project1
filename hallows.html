Although discussed earlier, I'd like to dive a bit deeper into All Hallows' Eve as it also has a tradition that carried over to modern Halloween.

While All Hallows' Eve is no longer observed by the Catholic church, it is still used as a preparation night for All Saints day. All Souls day comes after All Saints Day

Halloween was introduced to the United States when the British and Irish first came here, but wasn't widely celebrated until the 1840s during the Great Potato Famine, which brought an influx of people celebrating the tradtion. In the U.S., Halloween became a time for parties and pranking.
